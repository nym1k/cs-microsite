Originally released 24 June 2016, New Dawn is the debut album from UK progressive melodic death metal band Countless Skies. Eight tracks of atmospheric, melody-driven death metal with soaring clean vocals cutting through at the right moments: heavy where it needs to be, expansive where it counts.

Shaped by the Scandinavian melodeath scene, it sits somewhere between the bleakness and the anthemic, driven by hook-laden melodies, layered guitar harmonies, and a progressive ambition that gains momentum across the album.

To mark the 10th anniversary of its release, the band are re-issuing it independently for the first time. Every purchase goes directly towards funding the band and future releases.
